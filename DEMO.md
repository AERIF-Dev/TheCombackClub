# The Comeback Club — judge's walkthrough

Give what you can. Get what you need.

The hosted Site starts owner-private. Its hosting access gate may require ChatGPT sign-in; the interactive demo itself requires no application registration or AI credentials. To let judges visit independently, the owner must deliberately share the Site with them or request public access.

## The three-minute story

1. On the homepage, choose **Try Interactive Demo**. You enter as Marcus. The banner says **Demo Community**. All people and trades are fictional, and this browser has its own isolated session.
2. Open **My profile**. Marcus offers basic vehicle-care guidance and needs a website and garden help. You can edit his profile or add a listing.
3. Open **My matches**. Explore the **Comeback Circle** with Marcus, Jasmine, and Derek. The arrows show Marcus → Jasmine (vehicle-care guidance), Jasmine → Derek (website), and Derek → Marcus (garden).
4. Choose **Build This Comeback**. Review/edit the scope and suggested timeline. **Approve my part & send proposal** saves a real D1-backed proposal and Marcus's approval.
5. Use **Exploring as** to switch to Jasmine. Open **My exchanges**, then **Approve this agreement**. It remains Proposed until Derek approves too.
6. Switch to Derek and approve. The exchange becomes Accepted; it is not completed, and impact hours are still zero.
7. Choose **Start this exchange**. Confirm Derek's part is complete. Switch to Jasmine and confirm her part, then Marcus and confirm his part.
8. Open **Community impact**: one proposal, one accepted exchange, one completed exchange, one circle proposed, and **6 hours** exchanged. Reload: those records persist.
9. **Reset Demo** restores your fictional community without changing another visitor's demo or real member data.

## AI disclosure

Live model inference is not configured. Skill understanding, match explanations, scope/fairness guidance, and agreement drafts are explicitly rule-based. Real model request counts remain zero. A server-only OpenAI Responses adapter can be enabled later with authorized credentials; signing in with ChatGPT alone does not provide them.

The $0-payment concept is intentional. The app does not collect payments, claim vetting, expose precise addresses, or claim cash savings.
